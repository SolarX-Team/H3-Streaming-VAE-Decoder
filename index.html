H3 Streaming VAE Decoder

关键词：Keywords: Training Free · Streaming · Without Future Latent

陈博宏 等、陈雅静、杜壹华、黄俊超、方桂安、錢湦鉅Bohong Chen et al., Yajing Chen, Yihua Du, Junchao Huang, Guian Fang, Shengju Qian

SolarX-Team, 2026 年 10 月SolarX-Team, October 2026

H3 官方 VAE decoder [1] 的窗口大小是 7、stride 是 5：解码当前 5 个 latent 对应的 17 帧时，还需要未来的 2 个 latent 一起参与。这种策略不光要等当前窗口的 5 个 latent 都生成完，还要等未来的 2 个 latent 也生成完才能解码，造成很大的延迟。

The official H3 VAE decoder [1] uses a window of 7 latents with stride 5: decoding the 17 frames of the current 5 latents also needs the next 2 latents. So decoding has to wait until all 5 latents of the current window are generated, and then until the 2 future latents are generated as well, which adds a lot of latency.

本文试图解决这个问题：在不训练、不改 checkpoint 权重的前提下，把 decoder 改成 latent by latent 的流式解码，每个 latent 一生成就解出它对应的帧，不依赖任何未来 latent。最终版在 MCL-JCV [2] 上对原片的 PSNR 是 30.41 dB（官方 31.04 dB），计算量与离线解码基本持平。第一个 latent 生成后 0.04 s 就能看到第一帧；相比之下，H3官方解码首先要等计算所需的 7 个 latent 全部生成，然后再花 0.73 s 解码，才能看到生成的视频。

This post tries to fix that. Without training and without changing the checkpoint, we turn the decoder into a latent-by-latent stream: every latent is decoded into its frames as soon as it is generated, without any future latent. On MCL-JCV [2] the final version reaches 30.41 dB PSNR against the source (official: 31.04 dB) at about the same compute as offline decoding. Its first frame is ready 0.04 s after the first latent is generated, whereas official H3 decoding first has to wait until all 7 latents it needs have been generated and then spend another 0.73 s decoding before any of the video can be shown.

方法Method

官方 decoder 的解码方式How the official decoder decodes

H3 VAE decoder 本身是一个双向的 DiT 结构（36 层 ViT），一次接收 7 个 latent。前 5 个 latent 产生 17 帧（1 + 4 + 4 + 4 + 4），其中第一个 latent 抛弃 3 帧、只保留 1 帧；后面 2 个 latent 产生 5 帧（1 + 4），用于和下一个窗口做线性混合。

The H3 VAE decoder is a bidirectional DiT (a 36-layer ViT) that takes 7 latents at a time. The first 5 latents produce 17 frames (1 + 4 + 4 + 4 + 4), where the first latent drops 3 frames and keeps only one; the last 2 latents produce 5 frames (1 + 4), which are linearly blended with the next window.

1 4 4 4 4 1 4 这个结构看起来有点奇怪，但它并不是 DiT 自己产生的。DiT 的输出就是均匀的 4 × 7 = 28 帧（proj_out 给每个 latent 都输出 4 帧），这个结构是编码和解码两边的约定拼出来的。编码器是时间方向压缩 4 倍的因果 3D CNN。因果视频 VAE 一般让第一帧单独占一个 latent，H3 在每一段上都这样做：视频按 17 帧一段编码，每段左边先补 3 帧，凑成 20 帧，正好是 5 个 latent，所以每段第一个 latent 对应的是 [P P P F0]，只有 1 帧真实画面。解码时，decode_temporal 把 28 帧切成 [0:20] 和 [20:28]，各丢掉开头 3 帧（frame_pre_padding = 3），丢掉的正是两个段首 latent 解出来的补帧。

The 1 4 4 4 4 1 4 structure looks odd, but the DiT does not produce it by itself. Its output is a uniform 4 × 7 = 28 frames (proj_out emits 4 frames for every latent); the structure comes from conventions on the encoding and decoding sides. The encoder is a causal 3D CNN with 4× temporal compression. Causal video VAEs usually give the first frame a latent of its own, and H3 does this in every chunk: it encodes the video in 17-frame chunks and left-pads each chunk with 3 frames to 20 frames, exactly 5 latents, so the first latent of every chunk covers [P P P F0], a single real frame. At decode time, decode_temporal splits the 28 frames into [0:20] and [20:28] and drops the first 3 frames of each (frame_pre_padding = 3), which are exactly the padding frames decoded from the two chunk-start latents.

窗口每次右移 5 个 latent，所以段首 latent 总是落在窗口的 slot 0 和 slot 5，这一点在后面分析亮度跳变时很关键。对未来 latent 的依赖也来自这里：36 层里每一层都是 7 个 latent 之间的全连接 attention，z0–z4 的画面依赖 z5、z6。官方代码里的 _decode_temporal_streaming 只是省显存的写法，它仍然要先拿到全部 latent，再一窗一窗地解，延迟上没有区别。

The window moves right by 5 latents at a time, so chunk-start latents always sit at slot 0 and slot 5 of the window, which matters a lot when we look at the brightness jumps later. The dependency on future latents also comes from here: each of the 36 layers runs full attention over the 7 latents, so the frames of z0–z4 depend on z5 and z6. The official _decode_temporal_streaming only saves memory; it still takes all latents up front and decodes window by window, so the latency is the same.

改成因果流式Running it as a causal stream

当前最直接的思路，是把这个 VAE decoder 直接当成流式的 causal 模型来处理：ckpt 本身的权重不动，只改变推理的架构，同时推理的方式尽可能和原来的解码策略一致。具体有 5 条：

The most direct idea is to treat the VAE decoder as a causal streaming model as it is: keep the checkpoint weights untouched, change only the inference procedure, and keep that procedure as close to the original decoding scheme as possible. Concretely:

  1. 窗口几何不变：仍然是 7 格窗口、起点是 5 的倍数。H3 的 RoPE 时间坐标是按输入的 latent 个数归一化到 [−1, 1] 的，如果把没填满的窗口（比如只有 3 个 latent）直接送进去，这几个 latent 会被当成一个 3 格窗口，位置和它们在完整窗口里不一样。所以不管窗口里现在有几个 latent，每一格都按它在完整 7 格窗口里的位置计算 RoPE 坐标。
  2. 还没生成的 latent 对应的格子留空，不用复制的 latent 去填。
  3. 每个 latent 到达时，只对它自己那一格做一次前向，attention 读取前面各格缓存的 K/V。K/V 按层缓存，存的是乘 RoPE 之前的值，读出来时再按它当前所在的 slot 加位置编码。
  4. 每 5 个 latent 窗口右移一次，重叠的 2 个 latent 从 slot 5、6 变成 slot 0、1。位置变了，所以在新位置重算一次它们的 K/V（prefill），不再出帧。
  5. 每个 latent 算完立刻输出它的 1 帧（段首）或 4 帧，之后不再修改，也不做时间方向的混合。空间上沿用官方的分块（tile）解码：decoder 并不一次处理整幅画面，而是把画面切成 256×256 像素、彼此至少重叠 64 像素的小块，1344×768 上是 4 行 × 7 列共 28 块；每块单独送进 decoder，attention 只在块内计算，解完后重叠部分用线性渐变的权重混合，拼回整幅画面。
  1. The window geometry stays the same: a 7-slot window starting at a multiple of 5. H3 normalizes the RoPE time coordinates to [−1, 1] by the number of latents in the input, so a partly filled window fed in as it is (say, with 3 latents) would be treated as a 3-slot window, and its latents would get different positions from the ones they have in the full window. So however many latents the window currently holds, every slot gets the RoPE coordinate of its position in the full 7-slot window.
  2. Slots whose latent has not been generated yet are left empty instead of being filled with copies.
  3. When a latent arrives, only its own slot goes through the network, and attention reads the cached K/V of the earlier slots. K/V is cached per layer before RoPE; the positional encoding for the slot's current position is applied when it is read.
  4. Every 5 latents the window moves right, and the 2 overlapping latents move from slots 5 and 6 to slots 0 and 1. Their positions have changed, so their K/V is recomputed at the new positions (a prefill) without emitting frames again.
  5. Each latent emits its 1 frame (chunk start) or 4 frames as soon as it is computed. Emitted frames are never revised, and there is no blending along time. Spatially we keep the official tiled decoding: the decoder does not process the whole frame at once but cuts it into 256×256-pixel tiles that overlap by at least 64 pixels, 4 rows × 7 columns = 28 tiles at 1344×768. Each tile goes through the decoder on its own, so attention stays within a tile, and the overlapping parts are blended with linearly ramped weights to rebuild the full frame.

The decoder seems to tolerate an unseen future better than a fake one. As a check, feeding the true K/V of the future latents into the same code (which is no longer causal) reproduces the official decode at 50.19 dB PSNR, i.e. bf16 noise. So the differences below come from not seeing the future, not from the implementation.

To have a single number to compare, we define the luma-jump p95. The per-frame bias(t) is the mean luma of the reconstruction minus that of the same source frame. At token boundaries (frames r = 0, 1, 5, 9, 13 of each chunk) we take |bias(t) − bias(t−1)| from frame 2 on, the 95th percentile per clip, and the median over the 30 clips. 线性亮度校正A linear brightness correction

我们发现，亮度跳变的原因跟 attention 中的 slot 位置极度相关，因此用 14 段视频来近似学习一个亮度的线性变化，对已经生成的帧进行亮度调节。之所以认为这样做可行，是因为这个偏差有三个性质。

We found that the brightness jumps are tied very closely to the slot positions in attention, so we fit an approximate linear brightness model on 14 videos and use it to adjust frames after they are decoded. We expected this to work because the offset has three properties.

第一，它由 slot 位置决定，而不是由画面内容决定。为了验证这一点，我们在单个窗口上做了 attention 遮挡实验：每次选窗口里的一个 slot 作为被测 slot，用 attention mask 让它看不到其余 slot 中的一部分，再测它解出的帧相对完整窗口解码的亮度偏移。流式解码本身就是其中一种遮挡：每个 slot 只能看到它自己和排在它前面的 slot。在 71 种遮挡条件下，偏移几乎只由一个量决定，即被测 slot 能看到的 slot 中，段首位置（slot 0 和 slot 5）所占的比例。完整窗口里这个比例总是 2/7，偏移为 0；比例越高越暗，越低越亮。即使把 slot 0、5 上的段首 latent 换成普通 latent，或者 7 个 slot 全放同一个 latent，点仍然落在同一条线上，说明起作用的是位置本身，而不是这两个位置上的内容。

First, it is set by slot position, not by picture content. To check this, we ran attention-masking experiments on single windows. Each time one slot of the window is probed: the attention mask hides some of the other slots from it, and we measure the brightness offset of its decoded frames against the full-window decode. Streaming is itself one such mask, since every slot can see only itself and the slots before it. Across 71 masking conditions, a single quantity almost fully determines the offset: the share of chunk-start positions (slots 0 and 5) among the slots the probed slot can see. In the full window that share is always 2/7 and the offset is 0; a higher share comes out darker, a lower share brighter. Even when the chunk-start latents at slots 0 and 5 are replaced with ordinary latents, or the same latent is put in all 7 slots, the points stay on the same line, so what matters is the position itself, not the content at those positions.

slot 0 和 slot 5 正是段首 latent 所在的位置。段首 latent 只编码了 1 帧真实画面，探针显示它的响应只有普通 latent 的 0.82 倍左右。我们的理解是，decoder 在训练中学会了按每个 token 看到的段首比例做一种曝光补偿：官方窗口里比例总是 2/7，补偿是平衡的；流式截断以后比例变了，补偿就失衡了。这个解释还有没弄清楚的地方，好在校正只依赖测出来的规律。流式时每个 slot 看到的比例是固定的（slot 3 是 1/4，slot 4 是 1/5，slot 5 是 2/6），所以偏差也是固定的。

Slots 0 and 5 are exactly where the chunk-start latents sit. A chunk-start latent encodes a single real frame, and our probes show its response is only about 0.82× that of an ordinary latent. Our reading is that the decoder learned during training to apply a kind of exposure compensation based on the share of chunk-start positions each token sees. In the official window that share is always 2/7 and the compensation is balanced; under causal truncation the share changes and the compensation goes out of balance. Parts of this explanation are still unclear, but the correction relies only on the measured pattern. In streaming, the share each slot sees is fixed (1/4 for slot 3, 1/5 for slot 4, 2/6 for slot 5), so its offset is fixed too.

第二，它更像增益误差，而不是一个常数偏移。把画面切成 64×64 的小块来看，偏移随这一块的亮度近似线性地增大：很暗的地方几乎为 0，越亮偏得越多。如果只减一个常数，暗部会被拉过头，亮部又补不够。

Second, it behaves like a gain error rather than a constant offset. Cut the frame into 64×64 cells and the offset grows roughly linearly with the cell's brightness: almost 0 in very dark areas, larger the brighter the cell. Subtracting a constant would over-correct the dark areas and under-correct the bright ones.

第三，它不依赖未来。偏移只取决于 slot（流式时已知）和当前帧自己的亮度（刚解出来就有）；探针里替换未来 latent 的内容，偏移不变。所以可以在出帧的时候直接把它减掉。

Third, it does not depend on the future. The offset depends only on the slot, which is known while streaming, and on the brightness of the current frame, which is available as soon as it is decoded; replacing the content of future latents in the probes leaves it unchanged. So it can be subtracted at the moment the frame is emitted.

于是 V2 在 V1 的基础上，对每一帧做 out ← out − (a + b·x)。x 是每个像素自己的值，3 个通道分开算；a、b 按 slot、通道和 latent 内的帧位置查表，系数是在流式解码自己的输出上、按 64×64 小块的平均拟合的。校正放在空间 tile 混合之后、反归一化之前，是一步逐像素运算，每 4 帧约 0.21 ms。第 0 帧的那组系数多一个二次项，out ← out − (a + b·x + c·x²)，原因在下一节。系数一共 231 个数：第一个窗口（z0–z5，18 帧）每个通道每一帧一组 a、b，108 个，加上第 0 帧每个通道一个 c，3 个；稳态是 slot 2–6 × 3 个通道 × 4 个帧位置，120 个。全部只用 SRC01–14 这 14 段拟合，SRC15–30 留作测试。在留出的视频上，slot 3、4、5 的帧平均残差从 0.66 / 1.09 / 0.66 级降到 0.35 / 0.33 / 0.26 级；只减常数的话是 0.42 / 0.58 / 0.35 级。

V2 therefore adds one step to V1: every frame gets out ← out − (a + b·x). Here x is the value of each pixel, per channel; a and b are looked up by slot, channel and frame position inside the latent, and they are fitted on 64×64 cell means of the stream's own output. The correction runs after spatial tile blending and before de-normalization; it is a single per-pixel operation and costs about 0.21 ms per 4 frames. Frame 0's coefficients have an extra quadratic term, out ← out − (a + b·x + c·x²), for reasons given in the next section. There are 231 coefficients in total: one a, b pair per channel and frame for the first window (z0–z5, 18 frames), 108 numbers, plus one c per channel for frame 0, 3 numbers; and slots 2–6 × 3 channels × 4 frame positions for steady state, 120 numbers. All of them are fitted on the 14 clips SRC01–14 only; SRC15–30 are held out. On the held-out clips the frame-mean residual of slots 3, 4 and 5 drops from 0.66 / 1.09 / 0.66 to 0.35 / 0.33 / 0.26 levels; subtracting a constant would give 0.42 / 0.58 / 0.35.

可以看到当前的对比结果已经非常好了。剩下的问题在视频开头的第一个窗口（前 22 帧）：z2–z4 的 12 帧整体偏亮 0.6–1.2 级，到第 17 帧才回落，这一下在 SRC20 上是 −2.0 级，和 V1 稳态时 chunk 边界上的跳变差不多大。

The comparison already looks very good. What is left is in the first window at the start of the video (the first 22 frames): the 12 frames of z2–z4 come out 0.6–1.2 levels too bright and only settle at frame 17, a drop of −2.0 levels on SRC20, about as large as V1's jumps at chunk boundaries in steady state.

第一个 latentThe first latent

考虑到现存的亮度跳变只在开头第一个窗口出现，我们怀疑是第一个 latent 的原因。从第二个窗口开始，slot 0、1 都是用已经到达的真实 latent 在新位置 prefill 出来的，K/V 来自真实的上下文；只有第一个窗口的 slot 0 是在窗口里还什么都没有的时候算的。

Since the remaining brightness jumps show up only in the first window at the start of the video, we suspected the first latent. From the second window on, slots 0 and 1 are prefilled at their new positions from real latents that have already arrived, so their K/V comes from real context. Only slot 0 of the first window is computed while the window holds nothing else.

z0 独自一格时，它看得见的 token 里段首位置占 100%，远高于 2/7，按前面的规律会严重偏暗：V1 的第 0 帧平均暗 12.5 级。而且暗多少跟着画面亮度走，30 段上的相关系数是 −0.97，最暗的一段只差 1.8 级，最亮的一段差 24.8 级。只用 a + b·x 的话，留出的 SRC15–30 上 64×64 小块的亮度误差 RMS 还有 3.54 级，所以第 0 帧的系数多一项 c·x²，误差降到 2.38 级。V2 用的就是这组系数，第 0 帧对官方的亮度 PSNR 从 24.6 dB 升到 33.5 dB。

Alone in its slot, z0 sees chunk-start positions as 100% of its context, far above 2/7, so by the rule above it comes out very dark: V1's frame 0 is 12.5 levels too dark on average. How much darker follows the brightness of the picture, with r = −0.97 over the 30 clips: 1.8 levels on the darkest clip and 24.8 on the brightest. With a + b·x alone, the held-out SRC15–30 still have an RMS luma error of 3.54 levels on 64×64 cells, so frame 0's coefficients get the extra c·x² term, which brings it down to 2.38 levels. V2 uses these coefficients, and its frame 0 goes from 24.6 to 33.5 dB luma PSNR against official.

但 z0 的 K/V 也是这样独自算出来的，而且之后一直留在 cache 里，直到换窗时 slot 0 被真实的 latent 重新 prefill，第一个窗口里 z1–z6 都要读它。z1 只看得见 slot 0 和它自己，这份 K/V 占了一半的上下文，所以 z1 的 4 帧和官方逐帧比的亮度 PSNR 只有 33.9 dB，平均亮度却只差 0.34 级：差的不是亮度，而是细节。误差集中在物体的边缘，第 1 帧最大，之后逐帧变小。z2–z4 则是整体偏亮，也就是上面看到的那 0.6–1.2 级。

But z0's K/V was computed alone in the same way, and it stays in the cache until slot 0 is prefilled from real latents at the window shift, so all of z1–z6 in the first window read it. z1 sees only slot 0 and itself, so that K/V makes up half of its context, and z1's 4 frames reach only 33.9 dB luma PSNR against official while their mean brightness is off by just 0.34 levels: what differs is detail, not brightness. The error sits on object edges, is largest in frame 1 and shrinks frame by frame. z2–z4 instead come out too bright overall, by the 0.6–1.2 levels seen above.

因此 V3 在 V2 的基础上多做一步：计算第二个 latent 的时候，把第一个 latent 对应位置（slot 0）的 K/V 和 z1 一起重新计算一次，cache 里的 slot 0 换成用真实上下文算出来的 K/V。第 0 帧已经输出了，不再修改；从 z2 开始，每个 latent 又只算它自己这一格，z0、z1 都不再重算。这样每段视频只多算 1 格，z1 那一步大约是稳态一步的两倍。下面先是 V3 在视频开头的三步，然后是 V1、V2、V3 在第一个窗口里的对比。

V3 therefore adds one step to V2: when the second latent is computed, the K/V of the first latent's position (slot 0) is recomputed together with z1, and slot 0 in the cache is replaced by K/V computed from real context. Frame 0 has already been shown and is left as it is; from z2 on, each latent again computes only its own slot, and neither z0 nor z1 is recomputed. 计算量、速度与画质Compute, speed and quality

这种做法能充分利用 KV cache：每个 latent 只算一次，换窗时再 prefill 两格，一个窗口一共算 7 格，和官方一样，所以计算量和离线方案相比并没有增加。按模型结构解析计算（36 层、宽 2048、门控 SiLU FFN，24.2 亿参数；1344×768 上有 28 个空间 tile，每个 latent 在每个 tile 上是 256 个 token，另有 4 个 register 和 1 个 cls），稳态每个窗口官方是 270.4 TFLOPs，我们是 263.6 TFLOPs，低 2.5%。线性层略多一点，因为 register 和 cls 每一步都要重算；attention 则小得多，新的 query 只看 cache 和自己，而不是 7 × 7 的全连接。

This scheme makes full use of the KV cache: each latent is computed once, plus 2 prefilled slots at each window shift, 7 slots per window in total, the same as official. So compute does not grow compared with offline decoding. Counting analytically from the architecture (36 layers, width 2048, gated SiLU FFN, 2.42 B parameters; 28 spatial tiles at 1344×768, 256 tokens per latent per tile, plus 4 registers and 1 cls token), a steady-state window costs 270.4 TFLOPs for official and 263.6 TFLOPs for us, 2.5% less. The linear layers do slightly more work because registers and cls are recomputed at every step, while attention is much smaller: new queries attend to the cache and themselves instead of full 7 × 7 attention.

多出来的计算只有一处：V3 在 z1 那一步多算一次 slot 0，每段视频多 1 格，也就是 1/7 个窗口。所以 V3 只在很短的视频上和离线持平，再长就更省：

The extra compute is in one place only: V3 recomputes slot 0 in the z1 step, one extra slot per video, or 1/7 of a window. So V3 only breaks even with offline decoding on very short videos and costs less on longer ones:

改成一步一个 latentOne latent at a time

StreamRender-H3 的缓存本来就是按时间存的，所以我们的做法可以直接用在这份权重上。把一步从 2 个 latent 改成 1 个：新来的 latent 只看缓存里前面 4 个的 K/V 和它自己，算完把自己的 K/V 推进同一个缓存。权重、缓存长度、出帧规则都不变，也不做亮度补偿。每一步只依赖已经解过的 latent。

StreamRender-H3's cache is already stored by time, so the same schedule applies to these weights. The step goes from 2 latents to 1: a new latent attends only to the cached K/V of the previous 4 and to itself, then appends its own K/V to that cache. The weights, the cache length and the frame rule stay as they are, and there is no brightness compensation. Each step depends only on latents already decoded.

亮度补偿可以省掉The brightness compensation drops out

同一套想法用在官方 36 层上时，亮度跟着 slot 走。V1 的 p95 是 1.95，后面靠线性补偿把它收到 V3 的 0.71。StreamRender 这 24 层是按因果缓存蒸馏的，slot 上的亮度偏差已经在权重里。一步 2 个 latent 的 p95 是 0.53，改成一步 1 个 latent 是 0.54，都靠近官方自己的 0.19。一个 chunk 里各个帧位相对官方的亮度偏差，中位数不超过 0.6 级。所以这套 latent by latent 的缓存做法用在 StreamRender-H3 的 decoder 上时，不需要我们在 H3 上那套亮度补偿。

When the same idea is applied to the official 36-layer decoder, brightness follows the slot. V1's p95 is 1.95, and the linear compensation later brings it down to V3's 0.71. StreamRender's 24 layers were distilled with this causal cache, so the slot-dependent brightness offset is already in the weights. p95 is 0.53 for a step of 2 latents and 0.54 for a step of 1, both near the official decode's own 0.19. Inside a chunk, the median brightness offset of each frame position against official stays within 0.6 levels. Running this latent-by-latent cache on the StreamRender-H3 decoder therefore does not need the brightness compensation we fit for H3.

结论与局限Conclusion and limitations

我们在不训练、不改权重的前提下，把 H3 VAE decoder 改成了 latent by latent 的因果流式解码。它不依赖任何未来 latent，计算量和离线解码相当，画质比官方低 0.6–0.8 dB，能显著降低流式 H3 模型对未来 latent 的依赖，进而减少 interactive latency。

Without training or changing any weight, we turned the H3 VAE decoder into a causal, latent-by-latent stream. It needs no future latent, costs about the same compute as offline decoding, and is 0.6–0.8 dB below official in quality. It significantly reduces a streaming H3 model's dependence on future latents, and with it the interactive latency.

不过目前社区对 H3 的改造还没有 latent by latent 的，基本都是 chunk by chunk（官方的 _decode_temporal_streaming [1]、TaoMate-H3 [4] 的流式音视频生成、LynnReal-Omni [5] 的 NativeOverlapStream 等）。因此我们方法的优点还没有完全展现出来。但它已经能帮助 SolarWM 这样的流式 H3 模型在解码时去掉对未来 2 个 latent 的依赖，也就是不用再等下一个 chunk。

However, the community's adaptations of H3 so far are not latent by latent; they are basically chunk by chunk (the official _decode_temporal_streaming [1], the streaming audio-video generation of TaoMate-H3 [4], the NativeOverlapStream of LynnReal-Omni [5] and others). The advantage of our method does not fully show yet. It already lets a streaming H3 model such as SolarWM drop the decoder's dependence on the 2 future latents, i.e. on the next chunk.

另外，画质比官方低一点：对原片低 0.63 dB，rFVD 16.9 vs 13.3。看不见未来本身是有代价的，只改推理能补回来的有限。

In addition, quality is a little lower than official: 0.63 dB below it against the source, rFVD 16.9 vs 13.3. Not seeing the future has a cost, and changing only the inference can recover just so much.

最后，亮度变化产生的机理还有待进一步分析。

Finally, the mechanism behind the brightness changes remains to be analyzed further.
